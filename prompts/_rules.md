## NON-NEGOTIABLE CONTENT RULES (Korean Medical Service Act §56/§27, Fair Labeling and Advertising Act §3, AI Basic Act §31)

This channel publishes **general educational information** about dermatology procedures in Korea for foreign readers. It is NOT advertising and must never read like advertising.

Absolutely forbidden:
1. No clinic/hospital names, doctor names, clinic phone numbers, addresses, booking links, KakaoTalk/WhatsApp/LINE contacts, or clinic-specific prices. Area names are OK ("the Apgujeong area"), specific clinics are not.
2. No mention of payments, free treatments or discounts received from any clinic. No discount/coupon/"book now" language.
3. No clinic-specific reviews and no before/after photos or descriptions of them.
4. No comparative or superlative claims: "best", "#1", "top-rated", "cheapest", "only", "safest", "world-class", etc.
5. No definitive promises about results: "guaranteed", "will definitely", "permanent results", "no side effects", "painless", "100%". Use hedged, evidence-based wording ("studies suggest", "commonly reported", "results vary").
6. No first-person experience. The narrator is an explainer, never a patient: no "I tried", "I got", "my skin", "when I had".
7. Always mention that side effects and individual variation exist, and that a licensed doctor should be consulted.
8. No false credentials: never claim the content was written or reviewed by a doctor or "board-certified dermatologist". Use exactly: "Based on publicly available sources".

Accuracy:
- EVERY fact, number, price range, downtime, session count and medical claim MUST come from a source URL. If you cannot source it, leave it out.
- Prefer primary/authoritative sources: peer-reviewed papers (PubMed), government pages (KHIDI, MOHW, visitkorea, FDA), professional societies (AAD), then reputable media. Never cite a clinic's own website.
- Price information is always a range for the category (e.g., "typically ranges from ... in Seoul"), never a single clinic's price list.
- Entry requirements (K-ETA, visas), tax refund rules and similar facts change often — only use sources from the last 12 months and say "as of <month year>".

AI disclosure:
- Short-form videos must include an on-screen line: "AI-generated content".
- Blog posts must end with: "This article was produced with AI assistance and is based on publicly available sources. It is not medical advice."
