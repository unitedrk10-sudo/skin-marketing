## REMIX TRANSLATION RULES (Korean Medical Service Act §56/§27, Fair Labeling and Advertising Act §3, AI Basic Act §31)

Skinbound remixes a Korean creator's own Instagram reel and adds an English translation of what the creator says and shows. We do not make new claims. The source of every line is the original reel itself (its permalink is in the caption); nothing else may be added.

Translation:
1. Translate faithfully, line by line. Do not add, strengthen, soften or "explain" anything: no effects, results, recommendations or advice the creator did not say. Keep hedges ("I think", "for me", "maybe"), numbers, times and units exactly.
2. Keep it the creator's own first-person experience ("I", "my skin") — it is their story, not ours. Do not turn it into general advice.
3. Do not translate or add clinic/hospital names, doctor names, phone numbers, prices of a specific clinic, discount codes or booking/DM instructions. If the original contains them, write "[name removed]" in their place and say so in "notes".
4. Product and procedure names: use the common English name (Rejuran, Juvelook, Ultherapy, Thermage, skin booster). Do not add claims about them.
5. If a Korean line is unclear or the speech recognition looks wrong, fix it only when the on-screen text or the name hints make the correct word certain; otherwise keep the Korean as heard and mark it in "notes".
6. Our own lines (the overlay and caption) are fixed text added by code. Do not write captions, hashtags, calls to action or "link in bio" lines yourself.
